te-araroa.geojson - the Te Araroa trail
=======================================

The whole route of Te Araroa, one LineString per section (466 of them), as the GeoJSON the map draws.

  Source     Te Araroa Trust, "Tracks GPX", https://www.teararoa.org.nz/notes-and-maps/
             the 2026-27 season, file `TeAraroaTrail_2026_27.gpx` (7.59 MB, GPX 1.1)
  Copyright  Te Araroa Trust, 2026
  Licence    Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 New Zealand (CC BY 4.0)
             https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The app credits the Trust wherever the layer is drawn: the map's credit strip carries
"(c) Te Araroa Trust" linked to the Trust's own page while the layer is switched on, and the
Layers sheet's row names the Trust and the licence.

What was done to it, and nothing else: each `<trk>` became one LineString under the name the file
gives it, the `<wpt>` kilometre markers were dropped (the layer is the route, not 3,058 markers), and
the coordinates were rounded from the file's 15 decimal places to 6 - about 10 cm, against a trail the
Trust describes as mostly generalised. 466 tracks and 28,027 points in, the same out.

docs/te-araroa.md has the command that produces this file from that GPX.
